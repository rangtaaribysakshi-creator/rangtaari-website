A NOTE FROM SAKSHI

A love for Rajasthan,
made for everyday.

Rangtaari began with something simple — my love for the colours, prints and warmth of Rajasthan.

I wanted to bring that feeling into everyday homes. Not as something reserved for a special occasion, but as the bedsheet you reach for, the bag you carry, the kurti you wear and the gift you are happy to give.

So I curate pieces that feel distinctly Indian, joyful and useful — with Jaipur at their heart.

Sakshi Founder, Rangtaari by Sakshi
