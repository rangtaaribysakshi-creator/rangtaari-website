THE STORY BEHIND RANGTAARI

A little colour can change the feeling of a room.

Rangtaari is more than a shop.
It is my love for Rajasthan.

I wanted to create a space where the colours and character of Jaipur could become part of everyday homes — not only on special occasions, but in the little moments we live every day.

So Rangtaari brings together cotton home linen, practical bags, bath essentials, kurtis and gifting pieces inspired by the crafts, prints and joyful palette of Rajasthan.

— Sakshi
Founder, Rangtaari by Sakshi
