OUR STORY

Rangtaari started with a love for things that make a home feel happy.

Rangtaari by Sakshi brings Jaipur-inspired cotton home linen, lifestyle and gifting pieces to Mumbai and across India.

We believe Indian craft doesn't have to live only on special occasions. A printed bedsheet, a useful pouch, a bright tote, a soft towel — little everyday objects can carry colour, tradition and personality.
