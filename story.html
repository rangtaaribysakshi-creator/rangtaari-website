THE STORY BEHIND RANGTAARI

A love for Rajasthan,
made for everyday.

Rangtaari began with my love for the colours, prints and warmth of Rajasthan.

I wanted to bring that feeling into everyday homes — not only for special occasions, but through the things we use and enjoy every day.

From cotton home linen and practical bags to bath essentials, kurtis and gifting, I curate pieces that feel distinctly Indian, joyful and useful.
